# PatchUp — Product Overview

> Whole-class SEL and intercultural communication, ready to run.

PatchUp is a **teacher-first** platform for **whole-class social-emotional learning (SEL)
and intercultural communication** in K–12 classrooms (leaning elementary/middle). A
teacher signs up, picks a lesson, activity, or check-in, and runs it with the whole class
in minutes — students join with a simple code, no accounts required.

This document is the source of truth for what PatchUp is and who it serves. The marketing
landing page in this repo should reflect it.

---

## The repositioning (read this first)

PatchUp used to be pitched as a **division-bought, support-classroom data platform** —
the story was "deploy support programs and see whether they're working," with insights
rolling up to school admins and superintendents. **We are moving away from that.**

What changed:

- **From targeted support → whole-class.** It's for every classroom and every student,
  not just the support room.
- **From data-led → SEL-led.** We found the **data/measurement angle was not our best
  selling point — the SEL content is.** Data still exists as a *light, supporting*
  feature, but it is never the headline.
- **From division-bought → teacher-first.** We speak directly to teachers. The
  division/school hierarchy and top-down rollup story is **dropped** from the messaging.

## What PatchUp is for

Two **co-equal pillars** (give them equal billing in copy):

1. **Social-Emotional Learning** — whole-class SEL: emotions, empathy, self-regulation,
   relationships. The familiar, recognizable category teachers already look for.
2. **Intercultural Communication** — **building belonging in diverse classrooms.** Helping
   students from different cultural backgrounds understand each other, build empathy
   across differences, and feel they belong.

## Who it's for

- **Teachers** — the primary and only headline audience. K–12, leaning
  **elementary/middle**, but written so it doesn't exclude high school. They discover
  PatchUp, sign up themselves, and run it in their own classroom.
- **Students** — join a teacher's session with a **simple code, no accounts**
  (Kahoot-style), to do lessons, activities, and check-ins.

> Do **not** lead with divisions, superintendents, school admins, dashboards, or
> district-wide rollup. That hierarchy story is retired.

## Core concepts (content building blocks)

Three distinct building blocks — keep them distinct in all copy:

- **Lessons** — structured SEL / intercultural communication teaching. The deeper,
  guided content.
- **Activities** — shorter, fun, standalone exercises students actually want to do.
- **Check-ins** — quick "how are you feeling right now?" moments to take the temperature
  of the room.

> **Surveys are dropped.** They belonged to the periodic data-collection narrative we're
> moving away from — don't reference them.

## How it works

- A **teacher signs up free** and is running something with the class in minutes.
- The teacher launches a lesson, activity, or check-in.
- **Students join with a code** (e.g., `A3F9K`) — no login, no setup, no lost time.

## Data (deliberately minor)

Data is a **supporting feature, not the pitch.** SEL is the pitch. Keep any data framing
light and in-service of the teacher's classroom — never "analytics," "reporting," or
"progress over time at the division level."

> **OPEN QUESTION (TBD):** whether a check-in surfaces a *light, in-the-moment class
> snapshot* to the teacher, or whether there's *no teacher-facing data surface at all*.
> Not yet decided. Until it is, keep copy vague/neutral on this and do not promise either
> behavior.

## Adoption model & CTAs

- **Primary CTA: "Get started free"** — self-serve teacher sign-up. Teacher-first products
  live on low-friction sign-up.
- **Secondary CTA: "Book a demo"** — kept as a quiet link for the occasional school/team
  inquiry, not the main motion.

## Positioning notes (for copy)

- Voice: fresh, engaging, confident, **teacher-to-teacher.** (Do **not** mirror the older
  getpatchup.ca copy.)
- Lead with **whole-class SEL** as the recognizable hook and **intercultural
  communication** as the co-equal second pillar.
- Anchor value on the **experience in the classroom** — easy to run, no prep, students
  engaged, everyone feels they belong — **not** on data/measurement.
- Keep **Lessons / Activities / Check-ins** precise and distinct. No Surveys. No
  hierarchy/rollup language.
