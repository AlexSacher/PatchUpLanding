# PatchUp — Design System

> Brand feel: **calm, trustworthy, kid-friendly but professional.** A bright sky-blue
> gradient, a soft rounded wordmark, 
> Confident and clean — never corporate-cold.

---

## Logo

- **Wordmark:** `patchUp` — playful, rounded, bold lettering, lowercase with a capital
  **U**. Asset: `patchup-logo-white.svg` (white version, ~3.07:1 ratio, 1218×397).
- Used in white on the gradient. Needs a dark/colored variant for light backgrounds.

## Color palette

|---|---|---|
| **Primary / brand blue** | `#00A2E8` | Primary buttons, CTAs, active states. The signature PatchUp blue. |
| Deep blue | `#0A83B1` | Links, pill-button text, secondary accents |
| Body text | `#333333` | Default body copy / form text |

### Signature gradient

```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #66D6EA 0%, #4B6EA2 100%);
```
